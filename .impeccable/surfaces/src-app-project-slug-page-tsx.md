# Project case study

Scope: `/project/[slug]` for the four featured works. Visitor mode: Experience. The artifact leads. The homepage visual system stays fixed.

Audience: founders and product people deciding whether to hire Ashirvad. Job: judge the scope of one shipped product. Action: open the live site, move to the next record, or return to the index. Proof: the supplied splash, the stored client, budget, duration, year, stack, and description. Constraint: no invented screens, metrics, or testimonials. Budgets publish as stored.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A case study is a record of one shipped product. The splash occupies the sheet. The page refuses the case-study template of an eyebrow, metric cards, and pill tags.

OWN-WORLD: Field black sheet, JetBrains Mono, five dashed columns, index-white titles at section scale, graphite secondary copy, dashed rules, square plates. Client color only inside the image.

STORY: A founder sees the product first, then the client, budget, duration, and year, then what was built, then the stack, then the live site. They can move to the next record or back to the index.
