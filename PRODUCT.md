## Platform

## Users

Founders and product people deciding whether to hire Ashirvad Satapathy to design and build a product. They arrive at a personal portfolio, scan the featured work, open a case study, and judge whether the scope, craft, and working product fit what they need built.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Ashirvad Satapathy (dEvAshirvad), based in Bhubaneswar, India. It exists so a founder can see shipped client work and reach him. Success is a visitor opening a case study, judging real scope, and making contact.

## Positioning

Proof is a shipped product with a named client, a published budget, a duration, a stack, and a live URL. A portfolio of unnamed concepts or mockups cannot claim the same record.

## Operating Context

The site is one dark portfolio: a fixed header with local time and availability, a featured-work index, about, skills, FAQ, and contact. The four featured works are district-administration platforms. Case studies live on this site. The products themselves are hosted on client subdomains. Contact is email, GitHub, Instagram, and LinkedIn. The header states he is available for work.
